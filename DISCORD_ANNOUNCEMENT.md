## Medium Announcement (More Detail)

---

@everyone 🚀 **Major Announcement: Medi → Tolvex**

Hey everyone! We have some exciting news to share.

**Medi has been rebranded to Tolvex**, and we've just released **v0.1.6** to mark this milestone!

**Why Tolvex?**
As our project evolved, we realized our vision had grown beyond what "Medi" represented. Tolvex better captures our mission: empowering healthcare with secure, fast, and clinician-friendly analytics. The new name also provides stronger branding and trademark protection for our growing ecosystem.

**What's New:**
🔧 **Tooling Updates**
• Compiler: `medic` → `tlvxc`
• Package Manager: `medipack` → `tvx`
• File Extension: `.medi` → `.tlvx`
• Manifest: `medi.toml` → `formula.toml`
• Registry: `formulary.tolvex.dev`

🏠 **New Home**
• GitHub: https://github.com/Tolvex/tolvex
• Website: https://tolvex.dev
• Docs: https://tolvex.dev/docs
• X/Twitter: @TolvexLang

**What Hasn't Changed:**
✅ Healthcare-first design (FHIR, HL7, DICOM support)
✅ Compile-time privacy tracking
✅ HIPAA compliance by design
✅ Open source (MIT/Apache-2.0)
✅ Community-driven development

**Release v0.1.6**
This release includes complete rebranding across all tooling, documentation, and the codebase. All historical releases (v0.0.1 - v0.1.15) have been preserved in the new repository.

📖 **Full announcement**: [blog post link]
🔄 **Migration guide**: https://github.com/Tolvex/tolvex/blob/main/MIGRATION.md
📝 **Changelog**: https://github.com/Tolvex/tolvex/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md

**For Existing Users:**
Migrating is straightforward! Check out our migration guide for step-by-step instructions on updating your code and tooling.

**What's Next:**
We're launching the `formulary.tolvex.dev` package registry, expanding our healthcare standard library, and beginning pilot programs with healthcare institutions.

Thank you all for your support! We're excited to continue this journey as **Tolvex** and build the future of healthcare programming together. 🏥💻

Questions? Ask away in #general or #support!

— The Tolvex Team
